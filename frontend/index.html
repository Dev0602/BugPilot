Honest finding: PatchPilot and a one-shot Claude baseline (no sandbox, no tools, no retries) both achieved 100% repair success on this 20-bug benchmark. This indicates the benchmark's bug complexity was not sufficient to demonstrate a raw success-rate advantage for the agentic workflow. PatchPilot's demonstrated contribution is therefore execution-based verification, isolated sandboxed patch application, and structured retry handling — not a claimed improvement in raw repair accuracy.
